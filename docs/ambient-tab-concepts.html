The Ambient tab — your whole day, building something

A new tab next to Live: pick a display, leave it on a second monitor, and the day constructs itself in front of you. Every response feeds the scene; every hour leaves a mark; at midnight each scene gets a finale and a fresh canvas. Time is compressed — the demo day runs 8:00 → midnight in about a minute. The sky follows the clock.

day clock 08:00

And if we want to go even crazier:

  • Launchpad — the weekly limit is a rocket. Every session bolts on a part, fueling fills as the cap approaches, and actually hitting 100% is not a sad red bar… it's liftoff.
  • Aquarium — every open session is a fish; its size is its context window. Responses are food dropping in. A session that hits compaction visibly slims down. Close the terminal, the fish swims off-screen.
  • Vinyl — the day engraves grooves into a spinning record, groove density = token rate. Drag the needle onto any hour to watch that hour's pulse replay.
  • Accretion disk — tokens are stardust spiraling into a black hole that slowly grows all week. Bursts are meteor showers. The event horizon is your weekly cap.
  • Zen garden — each response rakes a line of colored sand; idle time slowly blurs it. A calm day literally looks calm.
