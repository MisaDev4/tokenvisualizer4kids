Token Town 3D — the session, as a real place

Same rules as the 2D MVP — every 5 minutes builds one house per model, it ranks up live at the construction site, solidifies, and joins the town; breaks plant trees; the sun is your actual sun. But now it's a 3D world with real lighting and shadows: drag to orbit, scroll to zoom, hover any building for its receipt. Backfilled ~4.7 h on load, exactly like the app would from its database.
